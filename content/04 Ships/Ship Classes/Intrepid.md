# Intrepid-class

## Overview

The **Intrepid-class** is a class of Federation starship designed for long-range exploration, scientific research, and rapid-response missions.
Developed by Starfleet during the late 24th century, the class combines advanced technologies, efficient crew requirements, and exceptional speed into a compact and highly versatile platform. The class is most famously represented by the [[USS Voyager]].
