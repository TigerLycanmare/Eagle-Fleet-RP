# Galaxy-class  

  

## Overview  

  

The **Galaxy-class** was one of Starfleet's largest and most powerful explorer designs during the late 24th century. Designed as a multi-mission deep-space exploration platform, the class was capable of carrying out scientific, diplomatic, humanitarian, and tactical operations with a high degree of autonomy. 【1-440469】  

  

The class is best known for the service of the **[[USS Enterprise (NCC-1701-D)]]**, commanded by Captain Jean-Luc Picard. 【1-440469】
