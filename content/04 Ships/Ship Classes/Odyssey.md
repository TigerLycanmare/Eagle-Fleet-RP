# Odyssey-class  

  

## Overview  

  

The **Odyssey-class** is a Federation starship class that entered service during the late 24th century. Designed as one of Starfleet's most advanced exploration and command platforms, the class represents the next generation of large multi-mission starships. 【1-3f350b】  

  

The class is best known for the **[[USS Enterprise (NCC-1701-F)]]**, which served as the Federation flagship during the early 25th century. 【1-3f350b】
