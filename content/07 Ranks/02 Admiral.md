# Admiral

## Overview

Admiral is a senior flag officer rank within Eagle Fleet. Admirals are entrusted with the command of major fleet formations, strategic initiatives, and key operational commands.
Officers holding this rank serve as senior leaders within the Admiralty and play a vital role in shaping fleet policy, readiness, and long-term planning.

## Responsibilities

- Commanding major fleet formations
- Directing large-scale operations and exercises
- Developing strategic and operational policy
- Supervising subordinate flag officers
- Managing fleet resources and readiness
- Advising the Fleet Admiral and Vice Fleet Admiral

## Authority

An Admiral holds authority over all subordinate officers and enlisted personnel assigned to their command and may issue directives in accordance with Eagle Fleet regulations and Admiralty guidance.

## Typical Assignments

- Fleet Commander
- Director of Fleet Operations
- Commander, Task Force
- Senior Admiralty Staff Officer
- Commander of a major sector or strategic region
