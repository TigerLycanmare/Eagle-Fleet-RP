# Commander

## Overview

Commander is a senior officer rank within Eagle Fleet. Officers holding this rank are typically assigned as executive officers aboard starships or starbases, command smaller vessels, or serve as senior department heads.
Commanders play a critical role in the day-to-day leadership and administration of Starfleet commands, acting as the link between senior command staff and junior officers.

## Responsibilities

- Serving as Executive Officer of a starship or starbase
- Commanding smaller vessels and installations
- Managing departmental operations
- Advising commanding officers
- Supervising junior officers and enlisted personnel
- Maintaining operational readiness and discipline

## Authority

A Commander holds significant command authority within their assigned role and may assume command of a vessel or installation when required by regulations or circumstances.

## Typical Assignments

- Executive Officer (XO)
- Commanding Officer, Escort Vessel
- Senior Department Head
- Operations Officer
- Strategic Staff Officer
- Training Command Staff
