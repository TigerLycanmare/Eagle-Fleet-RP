# Captain

## Overview

Captain is the highest non-flag officer rank within Eagle Fleet. Officers holding this rank are entrusted with command of starships, starbases, and other major installations, as well as significant administrative and operational responsibilities.
Captains are expected to demonstrate exceptional leadership, professionalism, and judgment, serving as the primary representatives of Starfleet and Eagle Fleet in their areas of command.

## Responsibilities

- Commanding starships and starbases
- Leading missions and operations
- Managing personnel and resources
- Maintaining readiness and discipline
- Representing Starfleet interests
- Mentoring subordinate officers

## Authority

A Captain exercises full command authority over their assigned vessel, installation, or unit and is responsible for the welfare, readiness, and performance of all personnel under their command.

## Typical Assignments

- Commanding Officer, Starship
- Commanding Officer, Starbase
- Station Commander
- Senior Staff Officer
- Academy Instructor
- Special Mission Commander
