# Lieutenant

## Overview

Lieutenant is a junior officer rank within Eagle Fleet. Officers holding this rank are experienced leaders and specialists who serve in key operational, scientific, engineering, medical, and command positions aboard starships and starbases.
Lieutenants are entrusted with significant responsibilities and are often assigned as division leaders, deputy department heads, and watch officers.

## Responsibilities

- Leading divisions and specialist teams
- Supervising junior officers and enlisted personnel
- Maintaining operational readiness
- Performing departmental management duties
- Serving as bridge watch officers
- Supporting mission planning and execution

## Authority

A Lieutenant exercises authority over assigned personnel and resources and may oversee departments, duty shifts, or operational functions as directed by senior officers.

## Typical Assignments

- Tactical Officer
- Security Officer
- Science Officer
- Operations Officer
- Medical Officer
- Engineering Officer
- Flight Control Officer
- Deputy Department Head
