# Fleet Admiral

## Overview

Fleet Admiral is the highest operational rank in Starfleet. Officers holding this rank are responsible for the command of multiple fleets, major strategic initiatives, and the overall direction of Starfleet operations.
Fleet Admirals are typically assigned to Starfleet Command, Federation strategic headquarters, or major wartime commands.

## Responsibilities

- Strategic leadership of Starfleet
- Oversight of fleet deployments
- Command of major task forces and fleets
- Coordination with the Federation government
- Authorization of major military and exploratory operations
- Appointment of senior command personnel

## Authority

A Fleet Admiral holds authority over all commissioned and enlisted personnel within Starfleet, subject to Federation law and civilian oversight.
