# Lieutenant Junior Grade

## Overview

Lieutenant Junior Grade is a junior officer rank within Eagle Fleet. Officers holding this rank have progressed beyond the entry-level responsibilities of an Ensign and are developing the experience, leadership, and technical expertise required for greater responsibility.
Lieutenants Junior Grade commonly serve as department officers, division officers, and bridge watch officers aboard starships and starbases.

## Responsibilities

- Leading small teams and divisions
- Supervising enlisted personnel
- Supporting departmental operations
- Standing bridge watches
- Assisting senior officers in mission execution
- Developing command and leadership skills

## Authority

A Lieutenant Junior Grade exercises authority over assigned personnel and resources and is expected to carry out orders while exercising sound judgement and initiative.
