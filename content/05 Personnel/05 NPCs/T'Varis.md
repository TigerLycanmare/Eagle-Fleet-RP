## Overview

Admiral T'Varis is a highly respected Vulcan officer and scientist who served for many years in both Starfleet and the Vulcan Science Directorate.
Known for a disciplined and analytical approach to scientific and tactical matters, T'Varis became involved in several highly classified research and intelligence programs during a distinguished career.
Although no longer serving on active duty, T'Varis remains an influential figure whose recommendations continue to carry considerable weight within scientific and intelligence circles. 【1-3e10b5】【2-7eb14d】

### Starfleet Mentorship Program

During Commander David Micheals' Academy years, T'Varis acted as a mentor and advisor. 【2-7eb14d】【1-3e10b5】
Areas of Guidance:
- Tactical reasoning
- Strategic analysis

T'Varis reportedly identified unusual neurological patterns in Micheals during advanced Academy simulations. 【1-3e10b5】

---

### Project Echoseed

T'Varis was among the first individuals to recognize that David Micheals displayed neurological characteristics inconsistent with standard Human baselines. 【1-3e10b5】
Following further investigation, T'Varis authorized a classified genetic review and recommended observation rather than direct intervention. 【1-3e10b5】
His standing recommendations have consistently been:
- Continued observation
- Non-disclosure to the subject
- Avoidance of forced activation
- Preservation of psychological stability
【1-3e10b5】【3-00c364】

## Relationship with David Micheals

T'Varis served as a mentor during Micheals' Academy years. 【2-7eb14d】【1-3e10b5】

## Known Philosophy

T'Varis is believed to hold the view that knowledge should be pursued with discipline and restraint.
