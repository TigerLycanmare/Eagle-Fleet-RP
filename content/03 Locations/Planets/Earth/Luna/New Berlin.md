# New Berlin

## Overview

New Berlin is one of the oldest and most prominent cities on [[Luna]], Earth's moon. Established during the early era of Human space colonization, it became one of the first permanent settlements beyond Earth.
By the 24th century, New Berlin was a thriving lunar metropolis and one of the most recognizable settlements in the Sol System.

## History

### Early Colonization

New Berlin was founded prior to the launch of the SS Conestoga and is among Humanity's earliest off-world settlements. 【1-449590】
The city played an important role in the expansion of Human civilization throughout the Sol System.

### 22nd Century

During the NX Program era, New Berlin was already a well-developed settlement and monitored much of the traffic between Earth and the Moon. 【1-449590】

### 24th Century

By the late 24th century, New Berlin had become one of Luna's largest population centers and was visible from Earth under favorable conditions. 【1-449590】【2-106858】
