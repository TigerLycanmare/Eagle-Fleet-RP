# Paris

## Overview

Paris is the capital city of France on [[Earth]] and serves as the political center of the [[United Federation of Planets]].
As the location of the [[Federation Council]] and the office of the [[Federation President]], Paris is one of the most important cities in known space. Diplomats, government officials, and representatives from hundreds of Federation member worlds routinely conduct business within the city.

## Historical Significance

Paris has remained one of Humanity's most influential cities for centuries.

### Pre-Federation Era

The city was an important political and cultural center on Earth long before Humanity achieved warp travel.

### Federation Founding Era

Paris played an important role in the administration of the newly formed Federation.

### Modern Federation Era

The city evolved into the center of interstellar governance and diplomacy.
