# Alpha Quadrant

## Overview

The Alpha Quadrant is one of the four major divisions of the Milky Way Galaxy used by most interstellar civilizations for navigation and political reference.
It is home to many of the galaxy's most influential powers, including the [[United Federation of Planets]], [[Klingon Empire]], [[Cardassian Union]], and [[Ferengi Alliance]].
Much of Starfleet's exploration, diplomacy, and military activity occurs within the Alpha Quadrant.
