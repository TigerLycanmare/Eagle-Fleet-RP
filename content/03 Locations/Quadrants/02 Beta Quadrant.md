# Beta Quadrant

## Overview

The Beta Quadrant is one of the four major divisions of the Milky Way Galaxy. It is home to some of the galaxy's most influential civilizations and contains portions of both Federation and Klingon territory, as well as the historic territory of the [[Romulan Star Empire]].
Unlike the Alpha Quadrant, which hosts much of the Federation's political infrastructure, the Beta Quadrant is often associated with major imperial powers, frontier exploration, and important strategic trade routes.
Several of the Federation's founding worlds, including [[Vulcan]] and [[Andoria]], are located within the Beta Quadrant.
