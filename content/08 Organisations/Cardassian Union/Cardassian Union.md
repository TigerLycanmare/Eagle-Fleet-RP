# Cardassian Union

## Overview

The Cardassian Union is a major interstellar state located within the [[01 Alpha Quadrant]]. Centered on [[Cardassia Prime]], the Union has historically been characterized by strong centralized authority, military influence, strict social order, and an unwavering emphasis on duty to the state.
Throughout the 24th century, the Union played a significant role in Alpha Quadrant politics, frequently interacting with the [[United Federation of Planets]], [[Klingon Empire]], [[Romulan Star Empire]], and later [[The Dominion]].

## Government

### Detapa Council

The civilian governing body of the Cardassian Union.
Responsibilities include:
- Civil administration
- Legislative affairs
- Foreign relations
- Domestic governance

### Central Command

The senior military authority within the Union.
Responsibilities include:
- Military operations
- Strategic planning
- Defense policy
- Territorial security
Historically, Central Command often exercised considerable influence over political affairs.

### Obsidian Order

The Union's intelligence and internal security organization.
Responsibilities include:
- Intelligence gathering
- Counterintelligence
- Internal surveillance
- Covert operations
The Obsidian Order was regarded as one of the most effective and feared intelligence services in known space.

---

## Cardassian Society

Cardassian culture places high value on:
- Duty
- Discipline
- Loyalty
- Family reputation
- Service to the State
Citizens are generally expected to place the needs of Cardassia above personal interests.

### Family Structure

Family loyalty is considered a cornerstone of Cardassian society.
Individual actions are often seen as reflections on the entire family unit.

## Major Historical Events

### Resource Crisis Era

Severe shortages helped drive the militarization of Cardassian society and government.

### Occupation of Bajor

A decades-long occupation of [[Bajor]] that became one of the defining periods of Cardassian history.

### Federation Border Wars

Numerous conflicts and disputes with the [[United Federation of Planets]].

### Klingon Invasion

The [[Klingon Empire]] launched military operations against Cardassian territory, severely weakening the Union.

### Dominion Alliance

During the [[Dominion War]], the Union aligned itself with [[The Dominion]].

### Cardassian Rebellion

Cardassian resistance movements eventually opposed Dominion control and assisted in ending the war.
