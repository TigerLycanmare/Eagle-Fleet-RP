# Daystrom Institute

## Overview

The **Daystrom Institute** is one of the Federation's premier scientific research organisations. Named after **Dr. Richard Daystrom**, inventor of the duotronic computer, the institute is responsible for advancing scientific knowledge and technological development across a wide range of disciplines.
The Institute employs both Starfleet personnel and civilian researchers and is considered a department within Starfleet.

## Areas of Research

The Institute conducts research in numerous scientific and technological fields, including:
- Cybernetics
- Robotics
- Artificial Intelligence
- Computer Sciences
- Propulsion Theory
- Physics
- Engineering
- Advanced Technologies
The Institute is organised into specialist divisions, including a **Cybernetics Division**.

## Notable Contributions

Throughout its history, the Daystrom Institute has contributed to numerous technological developments used throughout Starfleet and the Federation.
Notable achievements include:
- Research into advanced computer systems
- Development of emerging technologies
- Scientific publications and journals
- Advanced propulsion research
- Robotics and artificial life studies

## Notable Personnel

### Dr. Richard Daystrom

The Institute's namesake, Dr. Richard Daystrom, was the inventor of the duotronic computer and a pioneer in computer sciences and artificial intelligence research.

### Dr. Leah Brahms

Former student of the Daystrom Institute of Technology and renowned warp field theorist.

### Commander Bruce Maddox

Associated with robotics and cybernetics research at the Institute.
